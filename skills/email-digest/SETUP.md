# Setup — email digest

What the skill needs from your workspace.

## Required

**Read access to a mailbox.** Any mail tool, connector, or CLI the agent can drive — you connect and authenticate it yourself. Read is all it needs: the digest never sends mail and never modifies the mailbox.

**A place for the digest to land.** A file, a board card, a chat surface — wherever you will actually read it.

## Optional

- **A schedule**, once a manual digest has proved useful. Do not automate before that.
- **A preferences file** next to the installed skill, holding the people, projects, and deadlines that should always be surfaced.

## Check that it works

Ask for a digest over a window you already know — yesterday, or the last few days.

1. Is everything you remember as important in there, and near the top?
2. Can you get from a digest line back to the message it came from?
3. Is the mailbox untouched — nothing marked read, moved, or archived?

Say what was missing or noisy and have the skill adjust from that, rather than specifying the perfect digest up front.
